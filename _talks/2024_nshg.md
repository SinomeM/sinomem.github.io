I gave a short presentation at NSHG-PM 2024 on the on the development and application
of the CNN model that would later be published in [BMC Bioinformatics](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12859-026-06375-6). It's the same presentation I gave at EurBioc in Oxford that year.
You can find the slides of the presentation [here](https://sinomem.github.io/files/GW_CNVs_talk.pdf).

The talk covers also very quickly the application of the method to the UKB and the
Icelandic cohort at deCODE genetics. The results were never published but are
described in the four the manuscript of my [PhD thesis](https://sinomem.github.io/files/phd_thesis.pdf).
